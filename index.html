03 / The three-pass method

How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet.

Use a repeatable process instead of collecting links without context. These checks work whether you keep your shortlist in a spreadsheet or a simple notebook.

01.

Choose a category and purpose.

Start with one need: everyday sneakers, a layering hoodie, a jersey or a small accessory. Use the directory to browse relevant listings and save the original product links.

02.

Compare like for like.

Record the same fields for each candidate. Match the selected color, measurements, materials and included items before comparing prices. Keep seller claims separate from verified observations.

03.

Verify before payment.

Check availability, service compatibility, return terms and destination restrictions. Recalculate delivery costs and ask for missing information before committing to a purchase.

07 / Quick answers

Oopbuy spreadsheet questions, answered.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize item links, categories and comparison notes. Its contents depend on the publisher. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity, stock or compatibility with an agent.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent guide and category directory. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS. It does not provide Oopbuy account access, checkout or customer support.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

For shoes, compare foot length with the specific seller’s size chart. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. Do not assume that the same size label means the same measurements across sellers.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the selected color and size, visible condition, stitching, shape, labels and any requested measurements. QC photos can help reveal visible issues, but do not establish authenticity, comfort or long-term durability.

Does a listed product price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties separately. Final costs depend on the seller, service and destination.

Can every CAFANS item be purchased through Oopbuy?

Compatibility is not verified by this page. Check whether the original listing is supported by your chosen service, whether the item is available and whether shipping restrictions apply before paying.
